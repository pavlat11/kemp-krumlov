Pálava

The Pálava is the best-known inflatable canoe, suited to sporty river runs and touring. It offers enough room for two people plus gear for a week. The sporty kneeling position gives you perfect contact with the boat even in rapids up to grade WW III.

We recommend the Pálava for combined trips on the upper and lower Vltava – after the upper section you simply deflate the canoe and carry it packed. That saves you the cost of transporting boats around Lipno.

The Pálava is every bit as good as a classic fibreglass or plastic canoe, is half the weight, and thanks to its stability we can recommend it to beginners too.

Vydra

The covered bow and stern of the Vydra let you run rapids safely without taking on much water. Its big advantage is how manoeuvrable and easy to turn it is.

A two-seater touring canoe – easy to handle, nimble and stable. It is the most popular type of boat for family and sporty river trips.

Tukan

The Tukan is designed especially for touring. It can carry more luggage, a dog or a third person, for whom the boat can be fitted with a third seat.

Its large width adds to its stability. The buoyant tips increase its load capacity and keep it riding on top of the waves.
