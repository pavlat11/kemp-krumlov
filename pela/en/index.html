FAQ

Before you set off

Answers to the questions paddlers ask us most. Can't find yours? Give us a call.

725 336 601
What is included in the boat rental price?

Every canoe and raft comes with paddles, life jackets and dry barrels, plus a pump for topping up the raft if needed. If you book shuttle transport, the price also includes taking people and boats back to the starting point.

When are you open?

We are open from May to September, daily from 9 am to 7 pm. We take bookings by e-mail, phone or the form on this website.

Where can we start and finish on the Vltava?

We deliver and collect boats at Vyšší Brod, Rožmberk nad Vltavou, Nahořany, Camp Vltava, Viking / Pískárna, Vltavan / Spolí, Český Krumlov town centre, Zlatá Koruna and Boršov.

How much does shuttle transport cost?

The price per van for up to 8 people depends on the starting point – from €10 (town centre) to €46 (Vyšší Brod). See the price list for full details.

Do you offer group or long-rental discounts?

Yes. Rentals of 3 days or more get a 15 % discount. For groups of 20 or more, the group leader goes free.

Which boat is best for beginners or families with children?

Thanks to its stability, the Pálava inflatable canoe is suitable even for beginners. The Vydra is the most popular canoe for family trips, and the Tukan can take a third person or a dog. For larger groups we recommend the Bajkal (4 people) or Colorado (6 people) rafts.

Can you transport us around Lipno?

Yes, we transport people, boats and luggage from Lipno to Vyšší Brod.

How do I book a boat?

Fill in the booking form or call us on +420 725 336 601. Please tell us the date, place and time, type of boat, number of people and whether you need shuttle transport.
