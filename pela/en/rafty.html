Six-person raft

Colorado

The Colorado is a six-person family raft for river trips, with plenty of room for six passengers and gear for several days. It has also become a very popular racing raft in the Czech Republic – the R4 category is raced in the Colorado and, thanks to this boat, has become the strongest Czech rafting discipline.

Double air chambers in the main tubes keep you safe – if the outer wall is punctured, the inner chamber lets you reach the bank. Open stern – if the raft takes on water in the rapids, it drains out by itself.

Material: highly durable polyester fabric coated with synthetic rubber makes the raft very resistant to punctures.

Four-person raft

Bajkal

The Bajkal is a four-person family raft for river trips, with plenty of room for four passengers and gear for several days. It has non-slip seats made of waterproof plywood that keep the crew comfortable even on longer trips.

The Bajkal has a pair of mesh pockets on the inside of the side tubes – handy for small items you need on the way, and you can move them wherever you like.

Material: highly durable polyester fabric coated with synthetic rubber makes the raft very resistant to punctures.
